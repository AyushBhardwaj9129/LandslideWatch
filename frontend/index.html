About LandslideWatch

Smarter Monitoring. Safer Communities.

▲
LandslideWatch

LandslideWatch is an AI-powered platform designed to monitor and predict landslide risks in real time. It brings together environmental data, weather conditions and terrain analysis to help communities, authorities and emergency teams stay prepared and make faster, data-driven decisions.

By continuously tracking key factors like rainfall, temperature, humidity and soil erosion, the platform identifies areas that are more likely to face landslides before they happen, giving early warnings and actionable insights.

Our goal is to make landslide monitoring accessible, understandable and useful — helping to reduce risks, protect lives and build safer communities in vulnerable regions.

▦

The Risk Calculator

How We Calculate the Landslide Risk

The LandslideWatch Risk Calculator evaluates four key environmental parameters to estimate the likelihood of a landslide at a selected location. Each factor is given a weight based on its relative influence on slope stability.

☂
Very High Priority

Rainfall

40%

Contribution

Key factors

  • Rainfall intensity
  • Rainfall duration
  • 24-hour / cumulative rainfall
  • Soil saturation
  • Pore-water pressure
ⓘ Why it matters

Heavy and prolonged rainfall saturates the soil, increases pore pressure and reduces slope stability.

🌱
High Priority

Soil Erosion

30%

Contribution

Key factors

  • Topsoil removal
  • Slope weakening
  • Loss of vegetation / root support
  • Surface runoff
  • Gully formation
ⓘ Why it matters

Erosion removes soil and support, weakens the slope structure and creates vulnerable areas.

💧
Medium Priority

Humidity

20%

Contribution

Key factors

  • Atmospheric moisture level
  • Persistent wet conditions
  • Reduced evaporation
ⓘ Why it matters

High humidity keeps the ground moist for longer and, combined with rainfall, increases susceptibility.

🌡
Supporting Priority

Temperature

10%

Contribution

Key factors

  • Temperature variation
  • Influence on soil moisture
  • Weathering and material changes
ⓘ Why it matters

Temperature affects moisture levels and weathering, which can indirectly weaken slopes.

▦

Risk Calculation Formula

Risk Score = (R × 0.40) + (E × 0.30) + (H × 0.20) + (T × 0.10)

Where,

R = Rainfall E = Soil Erosion H = Humidity T = Temperature

(Each factor is normalized to a 0–100 score)
